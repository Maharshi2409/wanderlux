About WanderLux

A new Melbourne travel agency built on personal service, honest pricing and a love of exploring.

Our story

Why we started WanderLux

WanderLux was founded in 2026 by a small team of experienced consultants who believed holiday planning had become impersonal. Online booking sites offer endless choice but little guidance. We combine the convenience of online tools, like our instant Trip Cost Calculator, with the expertise of a dedicated human consultant.

Our mission

To design meaningful, stress-free journeys that match each traveller's budget, interests and pace, and to support them every step of the way.
